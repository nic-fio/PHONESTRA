The apps of your Android phone in windows on the Linux desktop, over Wi-Fi and without installing anything on the phone: a drawer with the apps, the notifications and the screen, each app in its own window with audio, mouse, keyboard, clipboard and files in both directions. GTK4 and libadwaita, distributed as an AppImage. It runs on Linux x86-64, with Android 14 or later phones.
